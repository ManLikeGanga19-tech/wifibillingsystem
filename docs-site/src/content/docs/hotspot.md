Hotspot is **prepaid Wi-Fi**: a customer connects, pays for a time or data bundle, and is online until it runs out. WIFI.OS handles the payment, provisions them on the router, and cuts them off automatically when the plan expires.

## How a customer buys

1. They connect to your Wi-Fi and are redirected to your **captive portal** (branded with your name and logo).
2. They pick a **[plan](/plans/)** — say *Daily Unlimited*.
3. They enter their phone number and get an **M-Pesa STK Push**. They enter their PIN.
4. On payment, WIFI.OS provisions them on the router and starts their clock. They're online.

If a payment callback is ever lost on the mobile network, a reconciliation check catches it within a couple of minutes and still connects the customer — so a paid customer is never left offline.

## Multi-device plans

A plan can allow more than one device (and dedicated **TV slots**). The paying phone can approve extra devices onto the same session with a tap, so a household on one bundle works the way people expect.

## Vouchers

Not everyone wants to pay online. **[Vouchers](/vouchers/)** are prepaid codes you generate in batches and sell in cash — the customer redeems the code on the portal instead of paying M-Pesa. Same result, no transaction fee.

## Watching sessions

The **[Active Users](/active-users/)** page lists everyone online right now. You can see their plan, device count, when their session expires, and disconnect anyone immediately if needed.

## What you need

- A **router** added under Settings → Routers, pointing customers at your portal.
- At least one **hotspot plan**.
- **Payments turned on** (verified M-Pesa settlement) so STK Push works.
