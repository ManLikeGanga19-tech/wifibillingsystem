title: Expenses
description: Record what the business spends so your reports show true margin, not just takings.
---

Takings aren't profit. The **Expenses** screen lets you record what the business actually spends — bandwidth, power, rent, fuel, salaries, equipment — so WIFI.OS can show you what you really keep.

## Recording an expense

Log an expense with its amount, a category and a date. Keep categories consistent — *bandwidth*, *power*, *transport*, *staff*, *equipment* — and you'll be able to see where the money goes month to month.

## Monthly totals

The screen totals your spend for the month at a glance, so you always know your running costs without adding up receipts.

## True margin

This is the point of it: with expenses recorded, your **[reports](/reports/)** show **true margin** — revenue minus real costs — instead of gross revenue that flatters the business. When you're deciding whether to drop a price or add a tower, you're deciding on real profit.

## Who can record expenses

Expenses are a finance function, so they're available to the roles allowed to handle money. Like every money-related figure, they stay inside your ISP and are never visible to another operator.
