The **Active Users** page shows everyone connected to your network **right now**, across every service type, in a single list.

## One table, every service

Use the filter at the top to switch between:

- **All** — everyone online, hotspot and broadband together, with a count per service.
- **Hotspot** — live prepaid sessions.
- **PPPoE** — broadband lines currently online.

Each row shows who they are, their plan, which router they're on, how long they've been online (or their session's expiry), and their IP.

## Hotspot sessions

For hotspot rows you can see the **devices** on a multi-device session (the paying phone plus any laptops or TVs the customer added), and you can **disconnect** anyone immediately — useful for abuse or troubleshooting.

## Broadband lines

For PPPoE rows you see the line's **uptime** and **WAN IP**. Broadband liveness comes from a check that runs every few minutes, so a line shows as online within a couple of minutes of connecting. To suspend or change a broadband line, use the **[Clients](/clients/)** page — those are billing actions, kept off the live list on purpose.

## Where else this shows

The same "everyone online" figure powers the **Online Now** tile on the **[dashboard](/dashboard/)** and the count on each router, so the number is consistent wherever you look.
