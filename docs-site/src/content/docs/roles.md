WIFI.OS keeps every ISP's data completely separate, and controls what each person in your team can do.

## Your data is yours alone

Your console shows **only your ISP's** customers, money and routers. There is no screen, filter, or link that can reach another ISP's data — the system is built so that isn't possible, not merely discouraged. Your customers' details and your revenue are never visible to anyone outside your business.

## People in your ISP

Everyone on your team gets a **role**, and the role decides which screens they see and what they can do:

- **Owner** — full control of your ISP: plans, customers, money, settings, and who's on the team. Some actions — moving money and changing access — are the Owner's alone and can't be delegated.
- **Admin** — runs the day-to-day: customers, plans, network, tickets. Everything except the Owner-only money and access controls.
- **Customer Care** — the front desk: look after customers, take payments, issue vouchers, raise and track tickets.
- **Technician** — the field: their assigned jobs, the map, and navigation to site. Their console is scoped to their own work.

People only see the parts of the console their role allows — a technician's menu is not a customer-care menu with things greyed out; the screens simply aren't there.

Money actions (payouts) always require a second factor, whoever you are.

## Access Control — tailor roles to your team

Roles aren't fixed. From **Settings → Access Control**, an Owner can decide exactly which permissions each role has — grant Customer Care the ability to issue refunds, or let an Admin see finance — and assign a role to each employee. Two permissions stay locked to the Owner no matter what: **moving money** and **changing access itself**, so no one can quietly grant themselves the keys.

Changes take effect immediately: if you remove someone's access, their next action is refused — there's no waiting for them to sign out.

## About Danamo (the platform)

WIFI.OS is operated by **Danamo Tech Ltd**. Platform staff cannot wander into your console. If you ask for help and they need to look, they enter through a **time-boxed, recorded** grant — and while they're in, a loud banner tells you so. When it expires, access ends. Every such session is logged.

The **[demo console](https://demo.wifios.co.ke)** uses this same machinery: it's a real tenant flagged read-only, so anyone can explore every screen while the system refuses every change. It's the honest way to see exactly what the product does before you sign up.
