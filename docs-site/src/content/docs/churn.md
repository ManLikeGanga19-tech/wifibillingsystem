For a broadband business, keeping customers matters as much as winning them. WIFI.OS tracks the **movement** of your subscriber base over time — not just who's active now, but who joined, who lapsed, and who left.

## The churn summary

On the **[Clients](/clients/)** page (and the dashboard) you'll see this month's:

- **New** — customers newly activated.
- **Suspended** — lines that lapsed for non-payment (they may still come back).
- **Restored** — lapsed customers who paid and reconnected.
- **Churned** — customers who left.
- **Churn rate** — the share of your base lost this month.
- A small **net-per-month trend** so you can see whether the base is growing or shrinking.

## What "churned" means

A broadband line moves through states: **active → suspended → cancelled**. A *suspended* line is overdue but not given up on. A *cancelled* line is one you've written off — it stops being billed and counts as churn.

## Cancelling dead accounts automatically

You don't have to chase every lapsed account by hand. In **Settings → PPPoE**, set **"Cancel suspended accounts after N days"** (e.g. 60). Any line that stays suspended that long without paying is automatically marked cancelled and its churn is counted. Anyone who pays before then is restored automatically. Set it to **Never** to decide by hand.

## Winning customers back

If a cancelled customer comes back and you re-provision them, WIFI.OS records it as a **reactivation** — so your numbers reflect the win-back, not a brand-new signup.
