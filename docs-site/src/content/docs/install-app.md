WIFI.OS installs like a native app. Add it to your home screen or desktop and it opens full-screen, with its own icon — no browser bar, faster to reach, and it keeps working smoothly on a patchy connection.

## How to install

**On Android (Chrome):** open your console, tap the menu, and choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

**On iPhone/iPad (Safari):** open your console, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

**On a computer (Chrome/Edge):** click the **install icon** in the address bar.

## One product, the right app for each person

Each part of WIFI.OS installs as its own app, with its own icon, so people install exactly what they use:

- **ISP Console** — the main operator console.
- **Field** — technicians get a separate app with its own icon, focused on their assigned jobs and navigation.
- **Customer Portal** — the connect-and-buy page your customers see.
- **Platform Control** — for Danamo platform staff.

A technician who signs in is offered the **Field** app to install; everyone else gets the Console. So the icon on a technician's phone is the field tool, not the full office console.

## Nothing to maintain

The app updates itself — whenever we ship an improvement, your installed app quietly picks it up the next time you open it. There's nothing to download from a store and nothing to update by hand.
