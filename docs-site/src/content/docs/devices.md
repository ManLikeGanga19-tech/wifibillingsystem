Your **routers** are where billing meets the network. When you suspend a customer in WIFI.OS, it happens **on the router** — not just in a database — because the console is actually connected to your MikroTik. The **Equipment** screen keeps track of the rest of your hardware.

## MikroTik routers

Connect each MikroTik router to WIFI.OS, and the console can provision and control it directly:

- **Provision a customer** — create their hotspot or PPPoE access on the router in one click.
- **Suspend and restore** — cut off a non-paying customer for real, and bring them straight back when they pay.
- **Stay in sync** — what the console shows and what the router is doing are kept aligned, so there's no drift between your billing and your network.

Give a router a location and it appears on the **[map](/map/)**, coloured by status, so you can see your devices in the field.

### Keeping credentials safe

Router credentials are stored encrypted and never shown back in plain text. The connection to your router is a management concern, handled carefully — see the security model in **[Security & sign-in](/security/)**.

## Equipment

The **Equipment** screen is your hardware inventory — radios, ONTs, antennas, cabling — so you know what you own, what's deployed, and what's in stock. Track equipment against sites and customers so a fault or an upgrade starts from an accurate picture of what's actually installed.
