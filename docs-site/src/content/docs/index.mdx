## What you can do with WIFI.OS

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Sell hotspot time" icon="rocket">
    Prepaid vouchers and M-Pesa time bundles, a captive portal your customers pay through, and automatic cut-off when a plan expires.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bill broadband (PPPoE)" icon="setting">
    Monthly fixed-line subscribers with anniversary billing, automatic suspension on non-payment, and one-click provisioning to your router.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Take M-Pesa" icon="approve-check">
    STK Push and Paybill (C2B) built in. Every shilling lands against the right customer, and the money is reconciled for you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="See the business" icon="bars">
    A live dashboard: revenue, who's online across every service, recurring revenue (MRR), collections and churn — updated as it happens.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Where to start

- New here? Read **[Getting started](/getting-started/)** for the five-minute tour.
- Want to look before you read? Open the **[live demo](https://demo.wifios.co.ke)** — it's a full, real console you can click around (nothing you do changes any data).

WIFI.OS is built by **Danamo Tech Ltd**. Each ISP gets its own console at `your-name.wifios.co.ke`.
