WIFI.OS is a complete platform for running an internet service provider in Kenya — from the moment a customer pays on the captive portal to the technician who drives out to fix the line. Billing, provisioning to your MikroTik routers, M-Pesa, a live map of your whole network, your fibre plant, and your field team — one console, one login.

## Sell and bill every kind of service

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Hotspot billing" icon="rocket">
    Prepaid vouchers and M-Pesa time bundles through a captive portal your customers pay on themselves. Plans cut off automatically when they expire, and one plan can be shared across a household's devices with tap-to-approve sharing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Broadband lines (PPPoE)" icon="setting">
    Fixed-line subscribers with **anniversary billing**, automatic suspension on non-payment, and one-click provisioning to the router — over fibre or radio, with full towers-and-sectors topology for the network behind them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="M-Pesa, built in" icon="approve-check">
    **STK Push** and **Paybill (C2B)** out of the box. Every shilling lands against the right customer and is reconciled for you — no spreadsheet, no manual matching.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Plans & vouchers" icon="list-format">
    Design your own hotspot and PPPoE plans, print or issue voucher batches, and let the front desk hand out access in seconds.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## See and run your whole network

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Live map" icon="random">
    Every tower, customer, router, lead and fibre point on one map of Kenya. Search any address, drop pins, colour customers by billing status, and see demand as a heatmap to decide where to expand next.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fibre plant & routing" icon="puzzle">
    Model your outside plant as a real network — OLT, splitters, ODPs, poles and the cables between them. Get **live port capacity**, a fault's exact **blast radius**, and the **shortest cable route** between any two points.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Field team & dispatch" icon="rocket">
    See your technicians' live positions and dispatch the nearest one to a job. Assign tickets by hand or automatically, and technicians get the location, directions and a phone app built for the field.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Real-time business view" icon="bars">
    A live dashboard: revenue, who's online across every service, recurring revenue (MRR), collections, arrears and churn — updated as it happens, not overnight.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Built for production

WIFI.OS isn't a script that runs one router — it's a multi-tenant platform built to run many ISPs at once, safely.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Your data is yours alone" icon="approve-check">
    Every ISP is a separate tenant. Your console shows **only your** customers, money and routers — there is no screen or link that can reach another ISP's data. Isolation is structural, not a setting.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Roles & real security" icon="star">
    Owner, Admin, Customer Care and Technician roles, tailored per ISP from **Access Control**. Moving money and changing access are Owner-only and can't be delegated, and money actions always need a second factor.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Changes reach the router" icon="setting">
    Suspend a customer and they're actually cut off on the MikroTik — not just marked unpaid in a database. What you see in the console is what's live on the network.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Install it as an app" icon="rocket">
    The console, the field app and the customer portal each install to a phone or desktop with their own icon, work full-screen, and update themselves — fast even on a patchy connection.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
