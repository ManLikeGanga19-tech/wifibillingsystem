A **plan** is a package you sell. There are two kinds, matching the two ways you sell internet.

## Hotspot plans

Prepaid time or data bundles for the captive portal. Each has:

- **Name and price** (e.g. *Daily Unlimited*, KSh 100).
- **Duration** — how long the access lasts (1 hour, 1 day, 1 week…).
- **Speed** — download and upload limits.
- **Data cap** — optional; leave empty for unlimited.
- **Shared devices** and **TV slots** — how many devices one purchase covers.

## PPPoE (broadband) plans

Monthly subscription packages for fixed lines. Each has:

- **Name and monthly price** (e.g. *Gold 15 Mbps*, KSh 3,500).
- **Speed** — download and upload.
- **Burst** — optional higher speed for short bursts, so browsing feels snappy.
- **Data cap** — optional; most home plans are uncapped.

The speed you set is enforced on the router automatically when a client on that plan is provisioned. Change a plan's speed and every client on it can be updated.

## Tips

- Keep your plan list short and clear — customers choose faster from three good options than ten.
- Price for M-Pesa: round numbers (20, 50, 100) are easier to pay and remember.
- You can retire a plan (mark it inactive) so it stops being offered without deleting the history of everyone who was on it.
