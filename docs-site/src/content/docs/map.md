The **Map** puts everything your ISP owns on one canvas of Kenya — towers, customers, routers, sales leads and your fibre plant — so you can see your whole network at a glance and decide where to expand next.

## What's on the map

Each layer has its own coloured pin, and you can toggle any of them on or off:

- **Towers** — your network sites.
- **Clients** — plotted where each customer lives; the colour carries their billing status, so a patch of red (suspended) or grey (churned) reads instantly.
- **Routers** — your MikroTik devices.
- **Leads** — prospects who've asked for a connection. Turn on the **Heatmap** to see demand as a density cloud — where to build next.
- **Fibre** — your outside plant (see **[Fibre plant & routing](/fibre-plant/)**).

Hover any pin for a details card. The card stays put while you move onto it, so you can click its links — including **Navigate**, which opens the location in Google Maps, Waze or Apple Maps for turn-by-turn directions.

## Search for a place

Use the search box at the top of the map to jump to any place or address — start typing and pick from the suggestions, exactly like Google or Apple Maps. Results are ranked nearest to wherever the map is currently looking, so local places come first. Picking one flies the map there and drops a pin.

## Find where you are

**Locate me** drops a live dot at your current position and follows you as you move — handy in the field.

## Your business location

Set your **business location** once (the prompt appears on the map, or use *"use my location"*) and the map always opens at your base of operations. Only an Owner or Admin can set it.

## Placing things on the map

A tower, client, router or lead appears on the map only once it has coordinates. Anything without a location is **counted, not hidden** — the map shows a banner like *"12 records without a location"* so nothing is silently invisible. To place one, open its edit page and either drop a pin, drag it, or use your current location.

We never invent coordinates for you — a customer's location is real data you set deliberately, so a suspended client with no pin simply waits to be placed rather than appearing somewhere wrong.
