The **Wallet** is where your money lives between your customers paying and you taking it out. Customers pay by M-Pesa, it lands against the right account automatically, and your balance builds up ready to pay out.

## Your balance

The wallet shows your current balance — money collected and reconciled, ready to withdraw. Because every M-Pesa payment is matched to a customer for you, this figure is real, not an estimate you have to reconcile by hand.

## Settlement — where your money goes

Set up your **settlement account** once — the bank or M-Pesa account your payouts land in. This is the destination for everything you withdraw, so you set it carefully and it's protected like any money control.

## Paying out

Withdraw to your settlement account when you want your money. Every payout requires a **second factor** — a one-time code from your authenticator app — no matter who you are. Moving money is never a single click, and the permission to do it is **Owner-only** and can't be delegated to another role.

## Statements

Download **statements** for any period — a clean record of what came in and what you paid out, for your books and your accountant. Your money history is always exportable and always yours.

## Expenses and true margin

Record what the business spends on the **[Expenses](/expenses/)** screen, and your reports show **true margin** — revenue minus real costs — not just gross takings. You see what you actually keep.
