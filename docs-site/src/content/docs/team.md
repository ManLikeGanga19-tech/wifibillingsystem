The **Team** screen is where you add the people who work in your ISP and decide what each one can do. Everyone gets their own sign-in and a **role** that shapes their console.

## Adding an employee

Invite a staff member with their name, phone or email, and a **role** — Owner, Admin, Customer Care or Technician. They get their own login; they never share yours. On first sign-in they set their own password.

## Roles at a glance

- **Owner** — full control, including money and access (some of it Owner-only).
- **Admin** — day-to-day running, minus the Owner-only money and access controls.
- **Customer Care** — the front desk: customers, payments, vouchers, tickets.
- **Technician** — the field: their assigned jobs, the map, and navigation.

Tailor exactly what each role can do from **[Access Control](/roles/)**.

## When someone leaves

Remove a person's access and it takes effect **immediately** — their next action is refused; there's no waiting for them to sign out. Staff are removed safely (soft-deleted), so their history — who did what — stays intact for your records even after they're gone.

## Accountability

Sensitive actions are recorded against the person who did them. Combined with per-person logins and roles, you always know who took an action, which is the point of giving everyone their own account instead of a shared one.
