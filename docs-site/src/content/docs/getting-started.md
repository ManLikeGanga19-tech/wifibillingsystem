WIFI.OS gives every ISP its own console at **`your-name.wifios.co.ke`**. Everything you do — plans, customers, payments, routers — lives under your own subdomain and is visible only to you.

## Signing in

1. Go to your console address, e.g. `homelink.wifios.co.ke`.
2. Enter your **phone number** and **password**.
3. You land on the **Dashboard**.

There's no separate app to install and nothing is stored in your browser — the session lives in a secure cookie, so signing in on a new device just works.

:::tip[Just exploring?]
The **[live demo](https://demo.wifios.co.ke)** is a complete console filled with realistic data. Log in and click anything — it's read-only, so nothing you do changes the data.
:::

## Your first day, in order

Setting up a new ISP usually goes like this:

1. **Add a router** — Settings → Routers. This is the MikroTik (or other gateway) at your site. WIFI.OS talks to it over a secure tunnel.
2. **Turn on payments** — verify your M-Pesa settlement details so money can reach you. Until you do, the console shows a banner explaining exactly what's left. See **[Billing & payments](/billing-payments/)**.
3. **Create plans** — the packages you sell (a 3-hour hotspot bundle, a Monthly 8 Mbps line). See **[Plans](/plans/)**.
4. **Start selling** — issue **[vouchers](/vouchers/)**, point customers at your captive **[portal](/hotspot/)**, or add **[broadband clients](/pppoe/)**.

## Finding your way around

The left sidebar is grouped by what you're doing:

- **Dashboard** — the health of the business at a glance.
- **Hotspot** — prepaid Wi-Fi: plans, vouchers, the portal.
- **PPPoE** — broadband lines: clients, invoices, plans.
- **Active Users** — everyone online right now.
- **Payments / Wallet** — money in, money owed to you, payouts.
- **Settings** — routers, messaging, billing, developer tools, security.

Badges on the sidebar show live counts (people online, open tickets, vouchers in stock) so you can see where attention is needed without opening each page.
