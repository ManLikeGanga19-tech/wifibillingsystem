description: Who can see and do what in your WIFI.OS console.

WIFI.OS keeps every ISP's data completely separate, and controls what each person in your team can do.

## Your data is yours alone

Your console shows **only your ISP's** customers, money and routers. There is no screen, filter, or link that can reach another ISP's data — the system is built so that isn't possible, not merely discouraged. Your customers' details and your revenue are never visible to anyone outside your business.

## People in your ISP

- **Owner** — full control of your ISP: plans, customers, money, settings.
- **Support** *(read-only)* — can see everything to help troubleshoot, but can't change data or move money.

Money actions (payouts) always require a second factor, whoever you are.

## About Danamo (the platform)

WIFI.OS is operated by **Danamo Tech Ltd**. Platform staff cannot wander into your console. If you ask for help and they need to look, they enter through a **time-boxed, recorded** grant — and while they're in, a loud banner tells you so. When it expires, access ends. Every such session is logged.

## The read-only demo

The **[demo console](https://demo.wifios.co.ke)** uses this same machinery: it's a real tenant flagged read-only, so anyone can explore every screen while the system refuses every change. It's the honest way to see exactly what the product does before you sign up.
