The dashboard is the first thing you see. It answers "how is the business doing right now?" in one screen, and it updates as money comes in and customers connect.

## The prepaid row

The top tiles cover your **hotspot / prepaid** side:

- **Revenue Today / This Month** — M-Pesa collected, with the month-on-month change.
- **Online Now** — everyone connected across **all** services (hotspot **and** broadband), with a breakdown like `90 hotspot · 52 pppoe`.
- **Success Rate (7d)** — how many payment attempts complete, so you can spot a paybill or STK problem early.
- **Total Clients**, **Avg Revenue / Client**, **Payments Today**, **Vouchers in stock**.

## The Fixed-line (PPPoE) row

If you sell broadband, a second row shows the metrics that matter for a **recurring** business:

- **MRR** — Monthly Recurring Revenue: what your active subscribers bill every month.
- **Collected this month** vs MRR — the gap is your collections problem in one glance.
- **Outstanding** — money owed to you right now (unpaid + overdue invoices).
- **Renewals due (7d)** — the value and count of subscribers billing in the next week: your expected inflow.
- **Active subscribers**, **New this month**, **Churn rate**, **Suspended**.

:::note
This row only appears once you have broadband subscribers, so a hotspot-only ISP isn't shown empty PPPoE tiles.
:::

## Charts and routers

Below the tiles:

- **Revenue — last 30 days** and **busiest hours**, so you can see trends and staffing peaks.
- **How clients pay** and **top plans by revenue**.
- **Sites & routers** — each router with its status and how many people are **active** on it, across every service type.

## Hiding the money

There's a **Hide** toggle that masks the revenue figures — handy when you're showing the screen to someone or working in public.
