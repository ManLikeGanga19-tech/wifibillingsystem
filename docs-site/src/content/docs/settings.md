Settings is where you configure how your ISP runs. Each panel is safe to leave on its defaults — you only change what you need. Here's every panel and what it's for.

## Profile

Your own name, contact details and preferences for your account.

## Branding

Your **name, logo and colours** on the captive portal and everywhere your customers see you.

## Domain

Your **subdomain** (`your-name.wifios.co.ke`) — the address your console and portal live at. Point your own domain here when you're ready.

## Payments

Connect **M-Pesa** — STK Push and Paybill (C2B) — so customers can pay and the money reconciles against the right account automatically. This is also where your **settlement** details live (see **[Wallet & payouts](/wallet/)**).

## Communications (SMS)

Choose and connect your **SMS provider** so messages and automatic notices actually send. See **[Communication](/communication/)** for what gets sent.

## Email

Connect email for statements, notices and welcome messages. Email proves and reaches the address; SMS is the quick, certain nudge.

## Hotspot & portal

Design the **captive portal** your customers buy on — pick from portal templates, set the look, and control the tap-to-approve device sharing, TV slots and shared-user budgets. See **[Hotspot billing](/hotspot/)**.

## PPPoE lifecycle

How your broadband book behaves:

- **Churn** — cancel accounts suspended beyond a threshold (see **[Churn & retention](/churn/)**).
- **Inactive prune** — tidy away long-disabled accounts (off by default).
- **Reminders** — SMS subscribers before their renewal falls due.
- **Invoicing** — auto-issue monthly invoices and set your invoice number prefix.

## Loyalty

Reward regular customers — customers **earn** as they pay, building repeat business. Turn it on and set how earning works.

## Message templates

Edit the wording of every **automatic notice** — welcome, renewal reminder, payment received, suspension — once, using placeholders like the customer's name and plan, so every future message sounds like you.

## Alerts

Choose which events you want to be told about, so the things that matter reach you without you watching the screen.

## Security

- **Change your password.**
- **Two-factor authentication** — an authenticator app, required to move money. See **[Security & sign-in](/security/)**.

## Developer

**API tokens** and integration tools for building on top of WIFI.OS — see **[Developer & API](/developer-api/)**.

## AI Assistant

The built-in assistant that helps you find things and answer questions about your console.
