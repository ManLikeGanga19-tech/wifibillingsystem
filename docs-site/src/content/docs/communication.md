WIFI.OS talks to your customers for you — the routine notices that keep them informed, plus the messages and campaigns you send yourself. It's all in the **Communication** section: **Messages**, **Emails** and **Campaigns**.

## Automatic notices

Much of your customer communication should just happen. WIFI.OS sends the routine notices — a payment received, a plan about to expire, an account suspended — so customers stay informed without you lifting a finger. You control the wording from **message templates** in Settings, using placeholders like the customer's name and plan so each message is personal.

## Messages (SMS)

Send an SMS to a customer or a group from the **Messages** screen. SMS is how you reach most Kenyan customers reliably — a payment reminder, a maintenance notice, a "you're back online." Sent messages are tracked so you can see what went out and whether it delivered.

## Emails

For customers with email, the **Emails** screen sends longer or formal messages — statements, notices, welcome details. Email proves and reaches the address; SMS is for the quick, certain nudge.

## Campaigns

**Campaigns** are bulk sends to a chosen audience — everyone on a plan, everyone in arrears, everyone in an area. Use them for price changes, new plans, or a coverage announcement. Each campaign tracks how many were sent and how many failed, so you know it actually reached people.

## Message templates

From **Settings → Message templates**, edit the wording of every automatic notice once, and every future message uses it. Set them up to sound like your business, in the language your customers use.

## Costs and control

Messaging uses your configured SMS and email providers (set in Settings). Sends are bounded so a mistake or a runaway can't blast your whole base by accident.
